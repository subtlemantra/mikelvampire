Seattle by Night

Chronicle Notes

What happened, who said what, and what you walked away with. Read it before the next session, because the city remembers even if you don't.

Session I

The Blood Moon Sacrament

Played Saturday, October 17 · St. Augustus Mission, Pioneer Square

  • Dr. Mari Valentineplayed by Allison
  • Séraphine de Montclairplayed by Jennifer
  • Marcus Rollinsplayed by Stuart
  • Abram Redmondplayed by Chris
  • Raymond Crossplayed by Thomas

The red moon risesThe sacrament begins

On the night of the eclipse, the moon turned the colour of old blood over Seattle, and the Camarilla gathered to mark it. Callus had lent the St. Augustus Mission for the sacrament. The shelter cots were gone, replaced by candlelit alcoves and a string quartet above the altar. Ghouls in black poured blood into cut-crystal goblets, and two hundred of the city's Kindred came to renew their loyalties in public. It was Elysium, so no violence was allowed, and the Harpy was always within earshot.

The coterie split up and worked the room.

The man nobody invitesDr. Mari Valentine and the Nosferatu

Mari found the Nosferatu standing alone in a tuxedo two sizes too big, with a carnation where an ear should be. Everyone else in the room had been avoiding him all night. She talked to him, and he was so grateful for it that he told her everything he knew.

Secrets learned

  • Hector's pipeline runs under the city. The Baron's people move product through the old Seattle Underground, beneath Pioneer Square. "They think nobody goes down there. I go down there." He knows where the drop points are.
  • Someone at the party is using. The big man by the font, with pupils like pinpricks, had been seen at the Square three times this month.
  • The Alastor went to Callus first. The night Cameron arrived in Seattle, he came up through the Nosferatu's tunnels. He didn't go to the Prince. He went to the Mission, to Callus.

A guest who drank too deepSéraphine, Marcus and the master of the Mission

Séraphine and Marcus sought out the man running the St. Augustus Mission and spoke with him. Séraphine drank too deeply of the blood he offered. The Beast woke, and she frenzied. She fed until she was satisfied, in front of the whole sacrament. Nobody was hurt, but the court saw it, and the court will talk about it for a long time.

Séraphine also left the sacrament with debts on both sides of the ledger. The Ventrue hostess owes her a boon. And Séraphine owes a boon to Callus, the Auditor. He has written both in his book, and sooner or later he'll come to collect.

Words from behind the maskMarcus Rollins and the Harpy

Marcus drew the attention of the Harpy, the masked keeper of the court's reputations. The Harpy changed him into his dog form and back again, then vanished into the crowd without giving up a single secret. Whatever the Harpy knows, they're keeping it for now.

All muscle, no filterDr. Mari Valentine and the enforcer

Mari also found the enforcer: six foot five, a tuxedo losing the argument with his shoulders, and twitchy for his next hit. He's the man by the font the Nosferatu had pointed out. He didn't seem to know which things were secrets, and he gave them all up.

Secrets learned

  • He's using. He was jonesing all night and kept asking where the lady in red's alcove was.
  • He's skimming the raids. "We hit one of the Baron's houses Tuesday. Took a cooler full of the good stuff. Most of it went in the evidence locker. Most of it."
  • His boss wants the Baron kept alive. "The boss says go easy on the Baron's runners. Bust a few, let the rest run. Says the Baron's more useful alive for now." He had no idea why that matters.

Something sweeter than the sacramentAbram Redmond and the seductress

Abram caught the eye of the most beautiful woman in the room, a Toreador in crimson silk with a rose-gold vial at her throat. She led him to a quiet alcove and offered him a taste of something sweeter than the sacrament: Hector's blood drug. Abram refused. With her charm spent and her offer turned down, she gave up everything she knew.

Secrets learned

  • She's selling the drug to the Camarilla's elite. She offers it to elders in quiet alcoves, and they keep coming back.
  • The drug comes in with the sacrament. Her supply is delivered to the Mission's service entrance, hidden inside tonight's vessel deliveries. "Where better to hide blood than in a party full of it?"
  • She's afraid of the Alastor. She's heard Cameron is asking about her. "If the Alastor asks, you never saw me. I can make that worth your while."

You've got mailRaymond Cross at the keyboard

While the others worked the room, Raymond slipped past the shelter cots into Callus's office, the little room behind the chapel where the lamp burns all night. There was a computer on the desk. He dialled in and logged into AOL. The web history was still there: someone had been researching a handful of Seattle clubs.

Teeth in the rainThe alley off Pioneer Square

The coterie left the sacrament with most of the night's secrets. A few blocks from the Mission, in a rain-slicked alley under a flickering sodium light, three werewolves were waiting. A grey war-form brute came down off the fire escape, a black wolf circled for throats, and a scared kid in a soaked hoodie named Toby shouted, "Which of you made it? Which one?"

It was a hard fight, and the coterie won it outright. All three werewolves lay dead in the alley. When it was over, Toby had left something behind.

Evidence: a matchbook

A crumpled matchbook dropped by Toby. The cover shows an open mouth baring one long canine tooth, with a drop of blood beneath it and the word Canine written over the drop.

Nothing was tucked inside it. It looks like the name of a club, and the AOL history on Callus's computer showed someone researching Seattle clubs.

Loose threads

  • Canine. What is it, where is it, and why was a werewolf carrying its matches?
  • The clubs on Callus's computer. Why was someone in the Auditor's office researching Seattle clubs, and is Canine one of them?
  • The Underground drop points. The Nosferatu knows where they are. Nobody has gone down yet.
  • The vessel deliveries. The drug comes in through the Mission's service entrance. Does Callus know what passes through his back door?
  • Cameron and Callus. Why did the Alastor visit the Auditor before the Prince?
  • The enforcer's boss. Someone in the Camarilla wants Hector alive, at least for now.
  • The Harpy. They know something. They chose not to say it.
  • Séraphine's frenzy. The court saw it, and the court remembers.
  • Séraphine's boons. The hostess owes her one. She owes Callus one. When will he call it in, and what will he ask for?
