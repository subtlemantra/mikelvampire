Seattle by Night

Dramatis Personae

Rain on the Sound, neon on wet asphalt, and a city whose nights are about to catch fire. These are the Kindred whose names you'll learn soon enough, whether you want to or not.

Baron Hector

Anarch Baron · Exile of Los Angeles

The fire under Seattle

In life, Hector moved product north out of South America, and he learned early that fear and supply are the same kind of power. Death didn't end his career. The Embrace just changed what he sold. When the Camarilla's court in Los Angeles fell, the new powers in that city drove him out along with everyone else who had grown too strong to ignore.

He came north with nothing but a reputation and a supply line. In the rain-dark corners of Seattle, he built again. His blood dolls grew hooked on the high of drug-laced vessels, and his ghouls were bound by blood and habit at once. The young Kindred he took in were neonates the city's elders had used up and thrown away, and he gave them a purpose and a fist to rally behind. They call him Baron, the Anarch title for one who holds a domain because he can, and because his gang lets him.

Hector preaches an old Anarch gospel: every vampire is equal under the curse. No Prince, elder or ancient Tradition gets to write the rules for the young. He enforces that equality the only way he knows, with violence. His Beast lives just under the skin, and a careless word in his presence has ended more than one conversation and more than one unlife. Now he means to unite Seattle's scattered Anarchs and break the Camarilla's hold on the city.

Cameron

Alastor of the Camarilla · 10th Generation

The rider who never stopped hunting

Cameron's family came from England with the westward tide: surveyors, rail men and settlers pushing the frontier toward the Pacific. Somewhere on that frontier in the 1800s, a vampire found him and decided that a man who could track a deer across bare rock and sit a horse through a blizzard would be useful for a very long time.

He was. As the railroads carried the kine west, the Kindred followed to claim the new cities, and Cameron rode ahead of them. He scouted the trails, guarded the routes and dealt with whatever waited in the dark. More than a century of that turned him into what the Camarilla calls an Alastor: one of the hunters sent by the sect's highest powers to track down those who threaten the Masquerade itself. Alastors answer to the very top, not to the local Prince. When one arrives in your city, someone important is already afraid.

Now Cameron hunts Baron Hector. The Camarilla doesn't much care about drugs. It cares about bodies, police reports and a trail of overdoses that leads back to things that should not exist. Cameron is patient, methodical and old-fashioned in his manners, and he is very hard to shake once he has your scent.

Callus

The Auditor · St. Augustus Mission

Every debt is written down

The St. Augustus Mission serves soup to the hungry, gives beds to the lost and asks no questions of anyone who comes through its doors after dark. Behind the chapel, past the shelter cots, a lamp burns all night in a small office. That is where Callus keeps his books.

Kindred society runs on boons: favours owed, repaid and traded between vampires, binding as any contract. Somebody has to keep track. Callus does. He audits who owes whom, which feeding grounds belong to which domain, and which promises were made in Elysium and quietly broken outside it. Princes have been embarrassed by his ledgers, and Barons have been ruined by them. The mission's endless stream of the forgotten also gives him something every Kindred needs and few can find so safely.

No one is entirely sure whose interests Callus serves, or whether his neutrality is real or simply the most valuable thing he sells. What everyone agrees on is that sooner or later, your name ends up in his book.
