The Clans

Your clan is your bloodline: the gifts you inherit, the curse that comes with them, and the urge the Beast pushes on you when Hunger wins.

The Core Rulebook covers seven Camarilla clans, the clanless Caitiff and the thin-blooded. The other six clans (Banu Haqim, Hecata, Lasombra, the Ministry, Ravnos, Tzimisce) and the Salubri appear in later sourcebooks, so ask the Storyteller before choosing one. Emblems here are original artwork for our chronicle.

Brujah

The Learned Clan · Rabble · Rebels · Philosopher-Kings

Rebels with a cause, or at least with a grudge.

The Brujah gather the angry, the idealistic and the overlooked: punks, activists, scholars, rioters, anyone who looks at the order of things and wants it torn down. Some are brawlers and some are theorists who want to understand a system before they break it. All of them carry a fire that turns dangerous fast. Their dream is a world without injustice; in practice, wherever they push for change, blood tends to follow.

Disciplines

  • Celerity — strike first, escape faster
  • Potence — end arguments with force
  • Presence — rouse crowds or scatter them

Bane · Violent Temper

Lose dice equal to your Bane Severity on rolls to resist fury frenzy (never below 1 die).

Compulsion · Rebellion

You must defy whatever counts as the status quo: your orders, your leader, someone's opinion. Until you do, you take −2 dice to all rolls.

Play one if…

You want passion, protest and fists, with a mind behind them.

Gangrel

Clan of the Beast · Outlanders · Ferals · Wolves

Closest to the animal inside, and proud of it.

The Gangrel are wanderers, hunters and outcasts who answer to no prince. They make their havens in the city's forgotten edges, the projects, highways and wild places, and they trust their pack over any court. Deeds count for more than titles with them: defend the pack, embarrass the powerful, survive the night. Of all vampires they live closest to their Beast, and over time it starts to show in their bodies.

Disciplines

  • Animalism — animal companions and spies
  • Fortitude — shrug off blades and bullets
  • Protean — claws, beast eyes, wolf and mist forms

Bane · Bestial Features

Each frenzy leaves animal traits (fur, a scent, a tic) equal to your Bane Severity. Each one lowers an Attribute by 1 until the next night. If you Ride the Wave, you take only one.

Compulsion · Feral Impulses

For one scene you take −3 dice to Manipulation and Intelligence rolls and can only speak in one-word sentences.

Play one if…

You want to be the survivor, the scout and the one who goes for the throat.

Clan chapter · p. 69Compulsion · p. 210

Malkavian

Clan of the Moon · Lunatics · Oracles · Children of Malkav

Their madness lets them see what everyone else misses.

Every Malkavian's blood carries a fracture of the mind: delusions, visions, compulsions, or perceptions that don't work the way others' do. The same crack lets in insight. Other Kindred have long sought them out as seers, advisors and court jesters who say the true thing no one wants to hear. Some are frightening, some are funny, and the useful ones are often both. Never assume a Malkavian doesn't know exactly what they're doing.

Disciplines

  • Auspex — visions, auras, reading minds
  • Dominate — bend or break a victim's mind
  • Obfuscate — the madman nobody notices

Bane · Fractured Mind

On a bestial failure or Compulsion, your affliction surfaces: −Bane Severity dice to one category (Physical, Social or Mental) for the scene. You and the Storyteller define the affliction at creation.

Compulsion · Delusion

For one scene your perceptions skew: −2 dice to Dexterity, Manipulation, Composure and Wits rolls, and to resisting terror frenzy.

Play one if…

You want strange insight, unsettling roleplay and real power behind it.

Clan chapter · p. 75Compulsion · p. 210

Nosferatu

Clan of the Hidden · Sewer Rats · Horrors · Lepers

Monstrous on the outside, often the most human within.

The Embrace twists every Nosferatu's body into something grotesque over weeks of agony. Shut out of polite society, they made the shadows their own: sewers, tunnels, data networks, and an unmatched trade in secrets. Information is their currency, and every clan eventually needs to buy some. Because they wear their curse openly, many Nosferatu keep a compassion that prettier Kindred have lost, and they are famously hard to kill off.

Disciplines

  • Animalism — rats, pigeons, swarms, messengers
  • Obfuscate — hide, or wear a borrowed face
  • Potence — hit hard, then vanish

Bane · Repulsiveness

You always have the Repulsive Flaw and can never buy Looks. Any attempt to pass as normal-looking, even with Obfuscate, loses dice equal to your Bane Severity.

Compulsion · Cryptophilia

You hunger for a secret. You take −2 dice to anything not aimed at learning one, until you uncover something worth knowing. You won't share it except in trade.

Play one if…

You want to be the information broker, the infiltrator and the survivor.

Clan chapter · p. 81Compulsion · p. 210

Toreador

Clan of the Rose · Divas · Degenerates · Artists

Beauty is power, and they are its connoisseurs.

The Toreador chase sensation: art, music, romance, fashion, and the darker kinds of beauty most people look away from. They are the patrons, performers and tastemakers of Kindred society, and they treat charm and desire as weapons. Write them off as shallow and you'll likely regret it. A Toreador can make even the dead feel something, and anyone who can make you feel can make you do almost anything.

Disciplines

  • Auspex — read moods, find the perfect vessel
  • Celerity — grace in art, terror in a fight
  • Presence — adoration, awe, an audience on demand

Bane · Aesthetic Fixation

In ugly or unpleasant surroundings, lose dice equal to your Bane Severity on Discipline pools. The Storyteller judges what counts, based on your character's taste.

Compulsion · Obsession

You become entranced by one beautiful thing: a person, a song, a pattern of blood. You take −2 dice to anything else until it's gone or the scene ends.

Play one if…

You want glamour, seduction, social power and decadence.

Clan chapter · p. 87Compulsion · p. 210

Tremere

The Usurpers · Warlocks

Mages who stole immortality and paid for it in blood.

The Tremere began as a medieval circle of mortal magicians who hunted for eternal life through horrific experiments, and found the curse of Caine instead. They rebuilt their lost magic as Blood Sorcery and spent centuries as a rigid, secretive hierarchy. That structure has since collapsed, leaving rival houses, scattered chantries and a clan trying to rebuild its power. Others still use them as scholars and occult experts, and most don't trust them.

Disciplines

  • Auspex — auras, clues, distant contact
  • Blood Sorcery — blood magic and rituals
  • Dominate — control minds and memories

Bane · Deficient Blood

Tremere blood can no longer Blood Bond other vampires (though Tremere can be bound). Bonding a mortal or ghoul takes extra drinks equal to your Bane Severity.

Compulsion · Perfectionism

Nothing less than flawless will do. You take −2 dice to all pools until you score a critical win on a Skill roll or the scene ends. The penalty drops to −1 when you repeat an action, and disappears on a second repeat.

Play one if…

You want occult knowledge, rituals and ambition.

Ventrue

Clan of Kings · Blue Bloods · Patricians · Tyrants

Born to rule, or so they insist.

The Ventrue are the aristocrats of the night: princes, CEOs, generals and power brokers who believe leadership is both their right and their duty. They uphold the Camarilla's laws and the Masquerade, often more strictly than anyone, and look down on clans they see as unruly. They run their bloodline like an old family firm, full of lineage, obligation and ancestors to live up to. Their blood is as refined as their manners, and just as demanding.

Disciplines

  • Dominate — command, erase, protect the Masquerade
  • Fortitude — the unbending lord
  • Presence — majesty and authority

Bane · Rarefied Palate

You can only keep down blood from one specific kind of mortal (e.g. soldiers, brunettes, the grieving). Feeding outside it costs Willpower equal to your Bane Severity. Resolve + Awareness (Difficulty 4+) tells you if someone qualifies.

Compulsion · Arrogance

Someone must obey you. You take −2 dice to anything that isn't leadership until a person follows an order you gave without supernatural help.

Play one if…

You want politics, command, influence and wealth.

Clan chapter · p. 99Compulsion · p. 210

Caitiff

The Clanless · Orphans · Freestylers

No family, no curse, no respect.

Some vampires simply don't take after their sire: the Blood of their clan never marks them. Most Kindred treat these clanless as trash or as a bad omen, but more appear every year, and they are becoming a real force. What they lose in pedigree they gain in freedom. No clan curse holds them back, and their blood can learn any power.

Disciplines

Choose any at creation. Every Discipline costs the same XP (new level × 6) once you've tasted the blood of someone who has it.

Bane · Outcast

There is no clan curse. You start with the Suspect Flaw, can't buy positive Status at creation, and other Kindred who know may give you −1 or −2 on Social rolls.

Compulsion

None of your own. Re-roll a clan result on the random Compulsion table.

Play one if…

You want a fully custom build and an underdog story.

Clan chapter · p. 105

Thin-Blooded

Duskborn · Mercurians · 14th–16th Generation

Barely vampires, and barely human anymore.

Thin-bloods are so far removed from Caine that the curse has nearly run out. Most vampires dismiss them, and some prophecies treat them as a sign the end is coming. They still need blood, but sunlight barely burns them, they pass for living far more easily, and some can even walk by day. In exchange they lack real vampiric power, so they make up for it with Thin-Blood Alchemy, brewing temporary powers from blood and emotion.

Disciplines

Thin-Blood Alchemy. Feeding also grants a temporary dot in a Discipline matching the victim's Resonance, lasting until your next feeding or Hunger 5.

Weaknesses

Blood Potency is always 0. You take damage like a mortal (blades and bullets are Aggravated), and a stake doesn't paralyse you, it just badly hurts you. You can't create Blood Bonds or reliably Embrace. Sunlight does only 1 Superficial damage per turn.

Compulsion

No clan Compulsion, and only supernatural means can push you into frenzy.

Play one if…

You want an outsider story, alchemy, and a foot in both worlds.
