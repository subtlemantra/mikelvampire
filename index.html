Enter the Darkness. Embrace the Night.

Behind every mask, a secret. Behind every secret, something far older than the night.

Seattle · 1998The rain never stops in the Emerald City, and neither does the hunger. Beneath the flannel and the feedback of a city lost in its own noise, something new moves through the veins of the Kindred: blood drugs. Vitae laced with mortal poisons, sweet as sin and twice as binding. It began in the gutters. Now it is sipped in the gilded salons of the Camarilla, rotting the old order from within while elders pretend not to notice. Drug lords rule the fringes the Prince no longer bothers to watch, and the Anarchs, long held at the city's edge, have found their foothold. Every addict is a crack in the Masquerade. Every debt is a weapon. Somewhere in the dark, the night is choosing its new masters.

The Lore

Long before the first candle was lit in this house, there were those who learned that a mask hides nothing — it only chooses what to reveal. Mikel gathered them, one night at a time, and the masquerade began.

Each gathering adds a page to a story that is never written down, only remembered. Guests come as strangers and leave as keepers of a secret. Some return. Some are never quite the same.

The rest is told in person, after midnight, to those who have earned it.
