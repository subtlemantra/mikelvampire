Blood, Hunger & Humanity

New Kindred usually start at Humanity 7 and Blood Potency 1 (0 for thin-bloods). The boxes below fill themselves in from your Blood Potency and clan, but you can change them.

Tenets, Convictions & Bane

Choose 1–3 Convictions, each tied to a Touchstone, a mortal who embodies it (e.g. "Clara, my sister: family is sacred"). Chronicle Tenets are the group's moral lines; Mikel will share them.

Advantages & Flaws

Spend 7 dots on Merits and Backgrounds and take 2 dots of Flaws, plus whatever your Predator type gives. Write one per line, e.g. "Haven" ●●, "Flaw: Prey Exclusion" ●.
